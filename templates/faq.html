Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about the calculator and its features.

Is Scientific Calculator 360 free?

Yes. It is free to use in a modern web browser, with no account required.

Can I switch between degrees and radians?

Yes. Use the Angle mode menu above the calculator. This affects sine, cosine, and tangent calculations.

Which functions are supported?

Basic arithmetic, parentheses, sin, cos, tan, square root, log, natural log, absolute value, factorial, powers, percentage, π, and e are supported.

Can I use my keyboard?

Yes. Type a mathematical expression in the display and press Enter to calculate. Press Escape to clear the display.

Are my calculations stored or uploaded?

Expressions are calculated in your browser. The local calculation history remains in the current page session and is not uploaded to the server.

Why might a result show rounding?

Browser calculations use floating-point numbers. Some decimal values cannot be represented exactly, and extremely large values can exceed numeric limits.

Does submitting a sitemap guarantee Google indexing?

No. A sitemap helps Google discover URLs, but Google independently decides when and whether to crawl and index each page.
