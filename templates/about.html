ABOUT US

Maths made more accessible

Scientific Calculator 360 is a free browser-based tool designed to make common scientific calculations easier to access on desktop and mobile devices.

What you can do

Use standard arithmetic, trigonometric functions, logarithms, powers, roots, constants, and factorials. The angle selector lets you choose degrees or radians for trigonometric calculations.

Accuracy and responsible use

Results use JavaScript floating-point arithmetic, so very large, very small, or precision-sensitive calculations may be affected by rounding. For safety-critical engineering, financial, scientific, or academic work, independently verify important results with an appropriate tool.

Privacy by design

Calculator expressions are processed in your browser and are not submitted to our Flask application. Standard hosting infrastructure may still record technical request logs.
